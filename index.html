RESEARCH PROTOTYPECagayan de Oro City · PhilHealth YAKAP labs

Smarter screening for early kidney damage.

YAKAP already covers HbA1c, creatinine, lipids, a blood count and a urinalysis. It does not cover the urine albumin test (uACR) that shows kidney damage early. NEPHLUX uses the labs YAKAP already pays for to help the doctor decide who should get that test first.

Screening support, not a diagnosis. Tested on U.S. (NHANES) and Korean (KNHANES) adults; not yet validated in Filipino patients.

Meet Nephra

Your guide to the app, never a doctor.

Nephra answers questions about NEPHLUX, YAKAP and the uACR test from a fixed set of checked answers. It is not a live AI, it never reads your results, and it sends medical questions to a YAKAP doctor.
